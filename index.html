Rules & Guidelines
  1. Whoever initiates the trade must send their link first.
  2. When requesting a trade, please make sure to send your full list.
  3. I am very busy these days, so I might miss your email or DM! Please don’t rush me, and if you don’t hear back after 3 days, feel free to message me again.
  4. I NEVER accept videos ripped from Bilibili! Videos encoded from Bilibili have terrible quality, and I think videos you can easily get with just a few searches aren’t even worth trading for!
  5. As mentioned above, I am currently not collecting non-Korean videos, so all videos on my list are ONLY traded for Korean videos!
  6. When declining a trade, please state your decision clearly! Otherwise, I’ll be left waiting indefinitely. Even just a super short two-letter “no” is totally fine, so please make sure to clearly let me know if you are declining!
  7. I NEVER want to trade with rude people. It’s basic common sense, so please always be polite!
  8. If you spot any incorrect information on my list, please feel free to let me know anytime!
  9. Never upload the videos you get from trading with me to social media!
  10. Never ask for NFT videos!
  11. Please trade any Korean videos you get from me only for other Korean videos. When adding them to your list, please make sure to state this clearly!
  12. Videos larger than 20GB have their file size listed separately! Any video without a listed size is under 20GB, but if you’re curious about the file size, please feel free to ask me!
  13. Korean musical videos don’t have a “master” concept, so there are no original notes provided by whoever filmed them! Because of that, I sometimes edit the notes however I see fit. If you’d rather not have that and want me to copy and paste the notes from your list exactly as they are, just let me know anytime!
  14. You’re welcome to copy the notes on my list as they are, but if you’d rather not, you don’t have to! (However, the statement that I only trade for Korean videos and the trade ratio must be kept. If you’d like to change the trade ratio, you may only change it to the ratio I’ve set or higher, in order to preserve the rarity of the material.)
  15. My list’s UI/UX is optimized for mobile! It isn’t optimized for PC screens, but all of the features work just fine!
  16. Korean videos filmed in 2024 or later will only be traded for videos on my Want List! Thank you for understanding!
